# Introductory GMT Cartography Exercises

The author's first plots made while learning **GMT (Generic Mapping Tools)**
scripting. The material mostly follows the official GMT tutorial together with
early practice exercises, and is kept here as a beginner-friendly learning
reference that may be useful to others starting out with GMT.

## What it covers

- `gmt psbasemap` frames and map layout
- Linear and logarithmic axes; annotations, ticks, gridlines and labels (the `-B` options)
- Titles and background colours
- Basic map projections — Cartesian (`-JX`), Mercator, Conic, Orthographic and Eckert — applied to the Kuril-Kamchatka Trench and the Pacific Ocean

## Contents

- `Pauline_GMT_tutorials.sh` — the GMT scripting exercises
- `GMT_tut_*.ps` — PostScript outputs of the tutorial steps
- `*.jpg` — rendered example maps (first map; the Kuril-Kamchatka Trench in Conic / Mercator / Orthographic projections; the Pacific Ocean in the Eckert projection)

## Requirements

GMT (Generic Mapping Tools) 5 or 6 (`gmt psbasemap`, `pscoast`, `psxy`, etc.).

## Note

These are early learning exercises rather than research figures; they are shared
as an educational reference for GMT beginners.
